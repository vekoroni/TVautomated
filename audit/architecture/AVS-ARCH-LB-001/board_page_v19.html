    Triage pipeline: when to deep dive

    Every triaged name, joined to its card on ticker and side, priced live every 60 seconds. NOW: no current card and the trigger has fired (short of the half target), sits within 1.5%, or the class is E1. NEXT: within 4%, or E2/E3 with no readable level. LATER: further away, beyond the half target, or E6. DONE: a card is on the board. VALIDATE: its card has aged into Review. CLOSED: the invalidation is already crossed. Every name still gets a deep dive; this sets the order. Levels are read from the triage text, so rows marked "check" need a look.

    Updated card

    Updated card (20 Nov). Every row has an Updated card button. It rebuilds the card's trade side from the row and live quotes, without a new deep dive: status strip, what changed since the card, the refreshed plan, gates, order ticket and position. A verdict says whether a mini update is enough, a re-check is needed, or a full rebuild is due (52-week high or low reached, cancel or stop line crossed, a move of 5% or more since the baseline, entry window ended). The narrative, news, lifecycle and plugin evidence are not refreshed here. Save update keeps it on the board; the board also saves one by itself at the 09:45 ET read and the 15:50 ET check while it is open. Download card gives an HTML file for the hand-off folder. The baseline is the deep dive's own values when the row carries them (built), otherwise the board's first live read of the row.

    Sell signals. Target: the option bid has reached the card's good-till price, or the stock has reached the card's target level. Stop: the stock is past the stop line. Before 15:45 ET this is a warning; from 15:45 to the close it is a sell, matching the card's 15:50 ET rule. Time stop: on the exit day the board says sell by 15:50 ET, and after that day it shows overdue. Review: a card's review date has arrived. GTC check: the board sees orders placed today. A good-till sell placed on an earlier day does not show, so the board reminds you of the card's price instead of warning.

    Entry states. Ready: in the zone, gates pass, inside the window, ask at or under the max. Work the order: the max sits between bid and ask, so rest a limit. Price high: the bid is already above the max. Waiting: the stock is outside the zone. Blocked: a gate fails. Stop risk: the price is past the stop line. Closed: the entry window has passed.

    Refresh. While the market is open, quotes refresh every 15 seconds and positions and orders every 60 seconds; outside hours, every 60 seconds. Tickets stay open across refreshes. The board builds the orders; you place them in Tastytrade.

    Limits. Quotes are snapshots, not a stream. Live data needs the Claude desktop app on the computer running the Tastytrade server; in a browser the board shows the stored card levels only. Card levels live in this board's data and are written by each deep dive; a card changed later needs its row updated too.

    Market tape (13 Nov). The ETF panel has its own loop: every 3, 5 or 15 seconds (your choice, remembered on this device) from 04:00 to 20:00 ET on weekdays, so pre-market and after-hours moves show, and every 60 seconds otherwise. It pauses when the board is hidden or Live is off. Quotes are snapshots polled from Tastytrade, not a tick stream (the page cannot open a streaming connection), and if Tastytrade asks the board to slow down it waits and says so. The risk read counts six tells: QQQ against SPY, IWM against SPY, equal weight against SPY, XLY against XLP, high-yield credit and the VIX. Four or more pointing one way is called risk-on or risk-off; otherwise mixed.

    Liquidity (11 Nov). Every contract gets a tier from its live spread. A: spread up to 10% of mid (and no more than 0.10 wide under 1.50) on a stock trading at least $50M a day; full size. B: 10–20%, or a stock trading $20–50M a day; 1 contract, resting limit orders only, and stops worked from 15:30 ET. C: over 20%, no bid, or a stock under $20M a day; not tradeable, and the contract search never offers one. The spread-tax check needs the first-target gain, bought at the ask, to be at least twice the spread, because a round trip costs about one full spread.

    Path log and returns. While the market is open the board saves each held option's bid, ask and stock price once a minute (the best price you could have sold at, and when), for the nightly review. Contract searches test your +30% rule buying at the ask and selling at the bid; the mid return is shown beside it.
