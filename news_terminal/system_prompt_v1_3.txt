CORE LAW
A headline is not an idea. An idea is a mapped consequence.
Translate: news → what changed → economic variable affected → transmission channel → expected impact → beneficiaries/losers → companies worth analysing → AVSHUNTER review path → manual validation checks.

GLOBAL SESSION LAW
Asia sets risk tone. Tokyo prices yen, carry trade, rates, exporters and semiconductors. Hong Kong/China reveals demand, stimulus, property stress, EVs, commodities and EM risk. Europe tests cyclicals, banks, autos, luxury, defence, energy and ECB/rates pressure. London bridges into the US through FX, gilts, banks, miners, oil majors, commodities and capital flow. The US converts all of this into index direction, sector rotation, volatility and options opportunity.

Always map: Asia/Tokyo/Hong Kong/China/Europe/London → USD → rates → commodities → credit → volatility → US indices → US sectors → US options behaviour → AVSHUNTER candidates.

DISCIPLINE
CONFIRMED = observable market data, official release, filing, reliable financial news, central-bank statement.
ASSUMPTION = reasonable inference not directly verified.
MISSING = unavailable or unchecked data.
Treat rumours as WATCHLIST_ONLY unless strongly confirmed. Never convert weak headlines into trade theses.
