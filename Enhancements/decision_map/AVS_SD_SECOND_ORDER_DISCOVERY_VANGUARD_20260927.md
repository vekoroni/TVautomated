# AVS-SD-SOR-001 — Second-order Discovery → Vanguard reasoning

**Status:** Proposed solution design for human review; review comments reconciled below. Not implemented, calibrated, or trading-authorised.

**Date:** 27 September 2026.

**Relationship:** Amends the C3/C4/C5 portions of the signed-off `AVS_SD_TICKER_FORECAST_TO_OPTION_VALUATION_20260927.md`. It does not supersede its C6–C14 option, valuation, governance, or release boundaries. Where this proposal changes a signed-off assumption, record an explicit design decision before implementation.

**Scope:** The underlying ticker reasoning chain, from dated market observations through dynamic structure and historical-path evidence to an option-neutral forecast. No broker order, capital authority, pipeline run, or database rewrite is authorised here.

## 1. Business decision and non-goals

The product has two separately testable outcomes:

1. **Ticker opportunity:** identify what changed in the stock, whether a plausible BULL, BEAR, or supported NEUTRAL_RANGE path follows, its possible magnitude and timing, structural trigger and invalidation, and what would revise that view. This is not a CALL/PUT recommendation.
2. **Expression opportunity:** given the frozen ticker forecast, search direction-compatible long options and determine whether any exact contract could monetise its paths after time, spread, IV, liquidity, and costs. A good ticker forecast may have no suitable option.

“Second-order” means evaluating **the consequences of a change in state and the market's possible response**, then revising the forecast when confirming or contradictory observations arrive. It does not mean adding an LLM vote, more static indicator points, or assuming that a 20-session number is a law of market development.

Wyckoff accumulation or distribution may persist for many sessions. The system must detect *development within the phase*, a suspected transition, confirmation, failure, re-accumulation/re-distribution, and a new range, without forcing a scheduled phase change. Every ticker gets an **observable market condition**; not every ticker has a defensible A–E Wyckoff phase. `PHASE_UNCONFIRMED` is truthful and is not silently converted to B/ACCUMULATION.

| Owner | New value object / event | Exclusive decision |

### 3.1 State is a dated episode, not a label on today's row

`StructureEpisode` key: ticker, adjustment convention, bar interval, detected range ID, engine version and as-of cut. It contains `condition` (trend/range/transition/insufficient), `mode_hypotheses` (accumulation, distribution, re-accumulation, re-distribution, no range), phase A–E *conditional on each mode*, origin, duration, prior trend, support/resistance frozen from bars preceding the tested event, event timestamps, control evidence, contradictions, and source-family lineage. Candidate stage is an observation, not a promise of direction.

At each new completed bar or authorised intraday observation, compare the episode with the previous one and append exactly one transition assessment: `PERSISTING`, `DEVELOPING`, `TRANSITION_SUSPECTED`, `TRANSITION_CONFIRMED`, `FAILED_TRANSITION`, `INVALIDATED`, or `NEW_RANGE`. The event includes old/new hypotheses and the exact evidence that changed them. A phase can persist while its maturity, compression, control and outlook change. Do not erase a failed spring, UTAD, breakout or forecast revision. Versioned, immutable events prevent an old value from being rewritten; **they do not alone prevent a partially published new value**. Publish a revision only after its source references, schema validation and downstream projection are complete, and test that the Lab never treats an in-progress revision as final.

### 3.2 Compression complements structure

Publish separately: NR7/NR4 from comparable **completed** bar ranges; 7/20 true-range and own-history percentile (clearly distinguish high–low range from ATR); inside-bar sequence; volume dryness; duration of coil; expansion trigger and whether expansion was accepted. `COILING`, `READY`, `RELEASING`, `NONE`, `DATA_INSUFFICIENT` are hypotheses with measured inputs and engine version. No fixed rule “low compression defeats Wyckoff.” Test interaction with mode, phase maturity and participation. NR7 alone does not establish a direction or an imminent profitable release.

### 3.3 Second-order paths and updating

The forecast starts with *mutually visible* paths, not one binary verdict. Example after a supported accumulation test: (a) reclaim→acceptance→upward expansion; (b) another support test or prolonged range; (c) failed reclaim→supply control→downward invalidation. A distribution test must have a mirrored set. Each path carries the confirming observation, contradicting observation, plausible move/timing range, and consequence for the ticker; only C6–C8 translate these into option economics.

**Estimator contract, inherited from the signed-off TEV-001 §4.1 rather than re-invented here:** the transparent baseline is a pooled, side- and geometry-aware competing-first-event table (Aalen–Johansen cumulative incidence is an eligible implementation). It estimates target-first, invalidation-first and event-free probability over observed sessions. A separate, duration-aware transition component describes `PERSISTING`/`DEVELOPING`/failed or confirmed phase change and survivor-return/move distributions; first-event incidence alone cannot supply a complete dynamic price path or option EV. The baseline must preserve gaps, ambiguous same-bar touches and missing paths as distinct typed observations. Reuse the existing C12 competing-risk semantics where equivalent; do not create an independent owner of the same outcome label. Neither the estimator nor its name repairs a bad range boundary or a legacy day-20 sentinel: construct and test the point-in-time labels first.

**Pooling and reliability are not new free parameters.** Use TEV-001's global direction-and-geometry parent, then supported sector/evidence-family adjustment, then supported ticker/feature adjustment. Record parent, local and parent counts, independent effective date blocks, tickers, sectors, shrinkage weight and exclusion reason. Keep the pre-registered `n_eff/(n_eff + kappa)` form; fit `kappa` on an earlier chronological calibration set and freeze it before the untouched cohort. A phase-only parent is prohibited because it can mix accumulation with distribution. Compression is measured separately and its interaction adjustment is zero unless the signed-off support rule and later-cohort incremental-value test pass; do not enumerate a full mode × phase × compression × macro grid. The provisional numeric floors remain **30 non-overlapping 20-session market-date blocks, 200 matured starts, 20 tickers and four sectors** for a pooled forecast; **12 blocks and 40 starts** for a local/interaction adjustment. A 95% block-bootstrap interval wider than 0.40 at a reported horizon is `POOLED_WIDE`. S0 must audit these floors without weakening them to obtain coverage. One episode with many daily rows is not many independent starts; overlapping ticker windows and common market-date shocks must be represented in the dependence blocks. A naive episode-level interval may be diagnostic only, never a publishable reliability label.

**Outcome clocks:** target or invalidation observed first is an event. A complete, event-free path through session 20 is an *observed non-hit through 20* and contributes to the survival mass at that horizon; an episode observed only through an earlier cut is right-censored there; a missing internal quote/bar or ambiguous same-bar order is separately typed under the pre-registered label policy. Do not call “horizon reached” a third competing absorbing event or count an unhit target as a day-20 hit. Report first-event incidence, survival, missing/censoring counts and intervals at supported horizons. The required uncertainty method must retain at least 20-session market-date dependence and sector/market-shock clustering; `(ticker, calendar-quarter)` alone is not sufficient. Present-but-thin or uncalibrated support is `STATISTICALLY_UNRELIABLE`/`POOLED_WIDE`, not `DATA_MISSING`, and a numeric research range is not a trade-ready probability. A decision-threshold-relative interval is not a substitute for calibrated coverage because C5 is not a binary trade gate.

Calibrated probabilities are optional outputs **only when held-out support warrants them**; otherwise publish qualitative scenarios with explicit reliability rather than fabricated precision. Compare a constrained discrete-time hazard or boosted-tree interaction challenger with the baseline on an untouched chronological cohort; no challenger is assumed better by model class. A duration-aware hidden semi-Markov model is later research, not an MVP dependency; no future smoothing is allowed at the historical decision point.

At Morning or a later cut, update from new evidence through an append-only `ForecastRevision`: prior forecast hash, observation cut, which path gained/lost support, changed timing or geometry, and whether the old thesis persists, develops, invalidates or is superseded. Treat OHLC-derived Wyckoff, compression, ADX and momentum as correlated evidence families, not four independent votes. An external dated sector or catalyst observation can change a path only with its provenance and as-of time. No observation rewrites the frozen Evening claim.

### 3.4 Time is a distribution, not one 20-day answer

The currently signed-off expression opportunity window is sessions 1–20. Estimate underlying development and resolution within it as a sequence, not a guaranteed horizon or a 20-session “hold.” Research may inspect intraday, 1/2/5/10/20 and 30/60/120-session outcomes when source resolution and independent support allow, but horizons beyond 20 **do not silently change** the signed-off C5/C6/C8 contract. They require a separate governed version and evidence that longer holding or different expiry economics is viable. A contract has its own forced exit before expiry; an early option exit does not invalidate a longer-lived ticker path.

## 4. Data integration and point-in-time controls

1. **Canonical bars** are the primary structure and ordered underlying-outcome path source; require completed-session status, as-of acquisition/revision reconstruction, adjustment parity, calendar sessions and no future bars in features. Intraday bars support auction claims only at their own timestamp and completeness grade.
2. **Actuarial v7** is retained untouched as a legacy comparison. Build a *new, versioned* episode/label panel by replaying the corrected live feature code on historical cuts and joining comparable forward paths. Do not map old Phase B fallback labels into the new phase ontology, and do not call `phase_v2` a Wyckoff phase.
3. **Phantom** supplies exact-contract quote/Greek and later option outcome evidence after the ticker forecast. It may contribute broker/market observations to auction only when exact time, symbol, convention and provenance checks prove the observation appropriate; option-chain interest alone cannot decide underlying direction.
4. **Macro/sector/news** are dated conditioning and counter-case evidence, not silent substitutes for ticker structure. Measured sector flow, inferred rotation and narrative are typed separately. Macro conflict changes scenarios and uncertainty; it does not rewrite a price observation.
5. **Hard prerequisite before any S3 comparable-set construction or model fit:** every versioned historical query requires an explicit `as_of` cut, with no silent default. A feature must have been available at the simulated episode start; a forward label must have been observed, acquired and computable by the training cut. A fully observed event-free horizon remains eligible as a non-hit; an open or interrupted path is not promoted to a matured label. Record each source's actual acquisition/revision availability and distinguish publication lag from the **at-least-20-session train/test overlap embargo** required by TEV-001. `ActuarialQueryEngine` currently has no such filter; either put a tested, fail-closed versioned boundary in front of it or change its consumers under a proved compatibility migration. No unfiltered legacy result may enter the new estimator. Replay tests must prove that later bars, late corrections and later-matured labels are excluded; freeze train/calibration/test cohorts before fitting.
6. Source-of-truth migration is conditional: run files remain immutable evidence until the journal stores hashes, snapshots/refs, clocks, model/config versions, rejected alternatives and outcomes and can deterministically reproduce their trader-facing fields. Neither database nor journal should overwrite a frozen past assessment.

## 5. Correctness invariants and counterexamples

- The same completed bars, adjustment convention and model version produce the same `StructureEpisode` regardless of run ID. A later bar may append a change, never alter yesterday's as-of label.
- A distribution Phase C is **not** accumulation merely because it is “C”; a Phase E of markdown is not markup. `mode × phase` is the structural key, with explicit no-range/uncertain modes.
- A current bar is compared with a range built from **earlier bars**. Synthetic tests for spring, UTAD, SOS/SOW, failed break and confirmed breakout must be reachable; the current all-history extrema defect must fail before repair and pass after.
- A genuine low-compression reading cannot erase otherwise valid Wyckoff evidence; compression changes the scenario's timing/energy assessment only where an empirically supported interaction exists. A Crabel state mismatch is a typed contract error, not silently `NONE`.
- Vanguard L1 `PROFILE_CONTEXT_ONLY` never becomes measured buyer/seller control, accepted migration, or execution readiness.
- Historical `EXACT` means exact on *declared, version-equivalent dimensions* with support counts. Broad/legacy analogue, omitted dimension or proxy is visible and cannot acquire exact-episode authority from sample size alone.
- For both BULL and BEAR geometry, target-first + adverse-first + observed event-free-through-horizon + early-censored + ambiguous/missing dispositions reconcile. A complete no-hit path through day 20 is observed survival through that horizon; an incomplete path is censored or missing at its actual last valid observation. Neither becomes a day-20 hit. Bearish probability is never derived from an upward-target column.
- Given the same `TickerForecast` envelope, C6 preserves run/thesis/ticker identity, BULL→CALL, BEAR→PUT and neutral→no directional expression regardless of the number of forecast scenarios. C8 receives the **entire weighted, time-indexed C4/C5 path set**, reconciles its target/stop/survival incidence and evaluates all same-side expressions on common paths; it may not collapse scenarios to a primary path or infer a better C5 direction from option economics. Version a changed C5 envelope and test existing adapters rather than silently changing its shape.
- A neutral or mixed signal cannot default to CALL. The C5 forecast has no `CALL`, `PUT`, OCC symbol, premium or option EV. `NO_SUITABLE_OPTION` cannot lower or delete the ticker forecast.
- Every update cites the previous forecast, new evidence, old/new paths, and explicit revision reason; Lab and Interpreter show the distinction between observed fact, model inference, scenario and missing evidence.

## 7. Evaluation protocol and business outcome

Pre-register the compared populations and all tested variants before viewing the held-out cohort. Evaluate, separately:

1. **Structural sensing:** agreement on blinded hand-labelled episodes, mode confusion, transition lead time, false alarms, persistence and range-boundary correctness; no forced phase assignments merely to increase coverage.
2. **Ticker forecast:** BULL/BEAR/range discrimination, first-target/first-invalidation timing, calibration where claimed, intervals and opportunity coverage; count missed large moves and false positives. Compare Discovery-only, corrected C3+compression, and added L1/L2/C5 on *identical* eligible starts with purge/embargo and date-block uncertainty.
3. **Expression economics:** exact-contract ask-to-bid outcome, unrealised but quoted gains, forced-exit sensitivity, transaction costs, IV/time sensitivity, no-option frequency, and human non-action. A correct ticker path with an unprofitable option is not a ticker-model failure; a profitable option quoted only retrospectively is not a filled trade.
4. **Operations:** final manifest, no stale-source substitution, source lineage, journal replay parity, Lab/Interpreter parity and safe fallback. Any numeric model that fails calibration remains labelled research-only.

The objective is a reliable shortlist of *explainable, monitorable and potentially monetisable* ticker paths, followed by a truthful choice of long option or `NO_SUITABLE_OPTION`. This design does not promise a target win rate, and it does not turn a historical paper return into execution authority.
