## 1. Decision and business outcome

AVSHUNTER has two sequential, separately testable products:

1. **Underlying forecast** — for each eligible ticker and completed evidence session, estimate the direction, plausible move *distribution*, timing, structural target if one exists, invalidation and uncertainty from Discovery, compression/Wyckoff, Vanguard layers 1–3, historical/actuarial evidence, and dated macro/sector/ticker context. This product contains **no option right, strike, premium or selected contract**. Contrary observations change the distribution or confidence; a low compression reading does not veto a Wyckoff observation.
2. **Expression assessment** — given that frozen forecast, search direction-compatible, exact long options (upward → calls; downward → puts), evaluate whether any can plausibly monetise the forecast after costs and timing, and explain the best supported expression or why none is currently suitable. No suitable option does **not** mean no underlying forecast.

The two outcomes must agree on `run_id`, `ticker`, `thesis_id`, direction mapping, evidence/quote as-of times and exit window. They need **not** both be positive. Morning appends observations and requotes; it cannot rewrite the Evening forecast. A changed underlying direction is a new thesis version, not a silent contract-side swap.

The initial production expression scope remains **long single-leg calls and puts**. Debit verticals and shares remain C8/C9 research comparators until separately governed; this design does not authorise short options, share orders, position sizing or broker writes.

Existing C0–C14 ownership remains, with these clarifications/amendments:

| **C5 Underlying Forecast** | `TickerForecast`: BULL/BEAR/NEUTRAL_RANGE with directional and survival probabilities where estimable, move range by session, target state/level, side-correct invalidation, trigger, uncertainty and opposing case; frozen Evening identity. | Name CALL/PUT, select an OCC symbol, use spread/OI/premium to decide whether the ticker has edge. |
| C6 Expression | Bounded `CandidateExpressionSet`; for BULL, long calls; for BEAR, long puts; each exact OCC identity, quote, expiry, exit policy and `last_exit_session`; rejection reasons per expression. | Change C5 direction, target, window or probability to make a contract fit. |

### Required C5 output (`ticker_forecast_v2`)

`run_id`, `ticker`, `thesis_id`, `evidence_session`, `as_of_utc`, `forecast_version`, `direction` (`BULL`/`BEAR`/`NEUTRAL_RANGE`), directional and survival probabilities or evidence-state with an explicit calibration status, move distribution by session 1–20 (not one asserted percentage), target state/level and derivation, structural invalidation and derivation, trigger and expected-resolution window, source-family IDs/timestamps, supporting and opposing observations, missingness, model/feature/policy versions, `forecast_state` (`QUANTIFIED`, `DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY`, `DATA_INSUFFICIENT`, `INVALIDATED`) and a **separate** `estimation_reliability_state` (`LOCAL_SUPPORTED`, `POOLED_SUPPORTED`, `POOLED_WIDE`, `STATISTICALLY_UNRELIABLE`, `NOT_ESTIMABLE`). A forecast with insufficient evidence is retained and labelled; it does not receive invented probabilities. `DESCRIPTIVE_ONLY` may be reviewed, but C8 cannot claim a numeric EV from it. `NEUTRAL_RANGE` is a positive, evidence-supported prediction of limited directional movement, not a synonym for missing data or option unavailability; C6 generates no long directional expression for it, while C11/C12 mature its underlying outcome.

No `CALL`, `PUT`, OCC symbol, premium, delta, spread, OI or `selected_contract` belongs in this contract. The existing `FrozenThesis` and CALL/PUT CSV fields remain behind a **versioned anti-corruption adapter** during migration; `BULL→CALL` and `BEAR→PUT` is a one-way C5→C6 mapping. Do not delete legacy fields or alter historical artefacts in place. A new `thesis_id` or explicit supersession is needed for a directional reversal.

### Required C6/C8 output

`expression_id`, parent `thesis_id`/forecast hash, OCC symbol/right/strike/expiry/multiplier, exact quote identity and timestamp, bid/ask and tradeability state, session-calendar-derived `last_exit_session`, exit policy, valuation/path-set/version IDs, per-path outcome and costs, EV in USD per share-equivalent and per contract, risk denominator, EV/R, stressed lower bound and RAEV **with authority status**, expected sessions held, and `NOT_VALUED`/`NO_POSITIVE_EDGE` reason. A quote is an estimate of executable economics, not a fill guarantee. Missing input is never `0`.

## 4. How the ticker forecast is formed without another blunt gate

1. Preserve the underlying observations from Discovery and all three Vanguard layers, Wyckoff, compression, historical outcomes, catalyst/ticker flow, and run-frozen macro/sector context with their actual as-of times. **This amends the older C0–C14 map that placed macro only in presentation**: macro may condition C5's forecast when it is point-in-time and independently sourced; it does not own ticker direction or act as a standalone veto. Record the source and whether sector flow is measured or inferred.
2. Prevent double counting: indicators derived from the same OHLC bars or the same option chain form evidence *families*, not separate votes. Conflicting families are published, not erased. Only source-integrity and invalid geometry make a numerical forecast unavailable.
3. Estimate direction, move size and time-to-resolution jointly through the **specified C4 estimator in §4.1 below**, not a Cartesian grid of indicator buckets. Complex models are challengers, not prerequisites; fit/calibration samples must be time-separated. A strong Wyckoff signal with low compression is a legitimate **combination to estimate**, not a fixed downgrade or approval.
4. Do not require a positive-EV option to declare a ticker forecast. Do not require every signal family to agree. If evidence is weak, keep the forecast visible with wide uncertainty or `DATA_INSUFFICIENT`; if a sourced stop is absent, retain the research row but mark it non-actionable until geometry is supplied. No synthetic 3R target or wrong-side stop.
5. C5 is frozen at Evening publication. Morning adds dated observations and may confirm, develop, invalidate or supersede; it never edits the old forecast or resets its 1–20-session clock.

### 4.1 Sparse-state C4 estimator: the quantitative core

**Unit and labels.** One observation is a ticker–completed-session–candidate-geometry start with its features frozen before that session's decision cut. For sessions 1–20, label first target touch, first invalidation touch, neither, ambiguous same-bar double touch, and genuinely censored/incomplete paths separately. A same-bar double touch follows the governed adverse-first convention; an unobserved day never becomes a survival day. The option right, quote, strike and premium are absent from this training table. The same historical start cannot be counted multiple times merely because several later runs rediscovered it; cluster together tickers sharing a market shock/date.

**No full-state cross-product.** Pre-register a small baseline feature set: direction/geometry distance in forecast-vol units, Wyckoff state and strength, compression as a continuous/ordinal measurement, one *measured and point-in-time* macro/sector context, and Vanguard evidence represented by independent source families. Do **not** enumerate every Wyckoff × compression × regime × sector cell. Fit competing target/stop discrete-time hazards for day 1–20 using a regularised pooled model with a low-dimensional day effect. Pre-register only the Wyckoff × compression interaction for the first challenger; add any other interaction only after time-separated incremental-value evidence. The per-day target and stop hazards plus remaining survival mass must sum to one; cumulative incidence must be non-decreasing. A pooled cause-specific estimator/Aalen–Johansen table is the transparent baseline against which the regularised model is judged.

**Explicit pooling/backoff.** Global direction-and-geometry hazards are the prior; sector/macro and evidence-family effects are *partial* adjustments, not a request for an exact-match ticker cell. Ticker-specific or cross-feature adjustments may influence the forecast only with adequate independent support. For an illustrative empirical-Bayes implementation, local adjustment weight is `n_eff/(n_eff + kappa)` and the residual weight remains on its parent; `kappa` is fitted on an earlier chronological training/calibration window, frozen before the test cohort. Backoff order is: exact ticker/feature adjustment → sector/evidence-family adjustment → direction/geometry pooled hazard. Record parent ID, local/parent counts, `n_eff`, shrinkage weight and exclusion reason. No nearest-state match or distance-grid snap is silent. If a measured macro feature lacks comparable historical releases, omit that effect and report it; never fill from current macro data.

**Pre-registered support floors, provisional until S0 audits their feasibility.** A *pooled numeric baseline* requires at least **30 non-overlapping 20-session market-date blocks**, **200 matured ticker-starts**, **20 distinct tickers**, and **four sectors** in its eligible training support. A local/ticker or interaction adjustment requires at least **12 independent date blocks** and **40 matured starts** for that adjustment; otherwise its weight is zero and the forecast uses the recorded parent. These are conservative engineering floors, **not published statistical guarantees or thresholds to tune on the test cohort**. In addition, report block-bootstrap intervals for target-first and stop-first incidence at days 5, 10 and 20. A **95% interval wider than 0.40 on the 0–1 probability scale (40 percentage points)** at a reported horizon is `POOLED_WIDE` even when count floors pass. S0 must report how many actual historical rows satisfy every floor. If the available data fail, mark `NOT_ESTIMABLE` or `STATISTICALLY_UNRELIABLE`; do not lower floors merely to obtain forecasts. A sensitivity analysis across pre-declared alternatives may inform a later version, never the untouched test result.

**Dependence and validation.** A 20-session outcome label overlaps adjacent starts. Independent support is therefore measured by 20-session non-overlapping market-date blocks with sector/market-shock clustering, not raw row count or a sum of correlated fallback states. Bootstrap and evaluation blocks must span at least the maximum 20-session label window; the older design's generic *week-block* bootstrap is insufficient unless an empirical dependence study demonstrates otherwise. Training, calibration and untouched evaluation are chronological; purge any label that overlaps a later evaluation start and embargo at least the 20-session label window. Record class calibration, Brier/log loss and interval coverage by direction and broad horizon; use the broad pooled forecast when a proposed refinement fails the later cohort.

**Move magnitude and neutrality.** Alongside first-passage hazards, estimate the conditional return distribution of surviving paths at each forced exit from the same point-in-time cohort, with partial pooling and volatility scaling whose parameters were fit before the decision cut. Keep tail/gap observations; do not replace a survivor's return with zero. Publish directional move quantiles and probability mass around a pre-registered economically meaningful range. `NEUTRAL_RANGE` requires sufficient support **and** a calibrated interval supporting high survival/limited directional movement across the relevant window; failure to find a BULL/BEAR effect alone is only `UNSUPPORTED`/wide evidence, not confident neutrality.

**Two quality axes, not one null.** Source/identity missingness yields `DATA_MISSING` or `NOT_VALUED_DATA`. Present but thin, highly dependent, shifted or uncalibrated history yields `STATISTICALLY_UNRELIABLE`/`POOLED_WIDE`, with a numeric *research estimate only if its provenance and interval are retained*; it cannot be relabelled as a calibrated EV. A supported but negative expected underlying move is `OPPOSED` or `NEUTRAL_RANGE`, not missing data. All states remain visible and mature outcomes.

## 5. Option search and EV correction

### 5.1 Expression generation before selection

For each BULL/BEAR forecast, including one labelled `POOLED_WIDE` or `STATISTICALLY_UNRELIABLE`, C6 enumerates a bounded, recorded set of **same-side** long contracts from a run-scoped completed chain (morning: fresh exact quote). A weak forecast may produce research candidates, but it cannot acquire a trade-ready numeric EV by virtue of finding a good-looking option. Bound enumeration by deterministic expiry/runway, strike/delta exploration and quote-tradeability limits; keep the full rejection taxonomy. `select_best_contract()` becomes a legacy proposal or comparison baseline, **not** the sole candidate supplied to C8. No option available yields `NO_SUITABLE_OPTION`, never `NO_TICKER_EDGE`.

### 5.2 One clock for forecast, one exit per expression

The ticker's opportunity window is sessions 1–20 from its frozen `window_start_session`. Each option's `last_exit_session` is `min(Day20, last permitted session before its expiry, configured time stop)` on the canonical exchange calendar, with the actual expiry buffer published. It may be 3, 7, 13, etc.; it is not inferred from a `1_5d`/`6_10d` label. An expression expiring before the forecast's likely move has a shorter monetisation chance; it does not shorten or invalidate the forecast. Keep DTE in calendar days and forecast/exit windows in trading sessions, with explicit conversion.

### 5.3 C8 canonical valuation

Use a common point-in-time underlying path set per ticker forecast and a path-specific exit at first target, first invalidation, or that expression's forced exit. **C8 consumes the C4 posterior hazards and survivor-return distribution from §4.1; it does not fit a second per-ticker probability model or re-shrink the same evidence.** Generate paths from the time-indexed, partially pooled physical-measure forecast, then reconcile simulated target/stop/survival incidence at every session against the immutable C4 packet within a pre-registered tolerance. Resample entire market-date/sector clusters with at least 20-session dependence blocks so candidate contracts share the same sampled underlying paths. Record pooled-parent lineage, effective independent blocks, path count, bootstrap interval, forecast error and calibration status in `path_set_id`. A ticker-specific path is an adjustment supported by data, **not** an exact-match claim about that ticker's repeated history.

Preserve gaps and the actual distribution of unresolved paths at forced exit. Reprice the *exact* option at each path's exit spot, remaining DTE and plausible IV dynamics; use ask-plus-explicit-cost entry and bid-like exit with observed/spread-model uncertainty, slippage and configured commissions. Keep separate base and adverse scenarios. Option IV/price is an observed market cost and scenario input, not an interchangeable real-world probability. Produce EV and an uncertainty interval only when the necessary inputs and supported C4 path packet are available. If market inputs are complete but the packet is `POOLED_WIDE`/`STATISTICALLY_UNRELIABLE`, publish a **shadow-only research range with reliability flag** or `NOT_VALUED_STATISTICAL_SUPPORT` according to the pre-registered precision policy; never disguise it as `NOT_VALUED_DATA` or a trade-ready EV.

The proposed lower-bound/RAEV rank is **shadow policy until validated**. A lower bound is not a ruin guarantee and must not set position size. Compare it against simple baselines (including current selected-contract economics) on held-out exact-contract outcomes before granting ranking influence. Do not hard-code an absolute spread floor as if it were a measured fill; calibrate it from observed exact-contract spreads or show it as a sensitivity assumption. Report probabilities, dollars and ratios with units and calibration state.

### 5.4 EV-3 disposition: retrospective diagnostic, not a parallel repair

**Do not build or maintain a second live EV-3 clock repair track.** S0 performs a read-only retrospective classification of the frozen `20260926_173730` receipt and a small offline fixture comparison, proving which rows fail the clock, thesis geometry, absent contract, quote and liquidity stages. This is enough to document the old defect. Leave existing EV-3 outputs namespaced and permanently non-authoritative during transition; a `ZERO_COVERAGE` status remains visible as legacy diagnostic evidence, not a surrogate for C8 progress. Once C8 passes its own end-to-end parity and governed cutover, disable EV-3's scheduled evaluation/consumer by versioned configuration (retain its code, historical artefacts and rollback capability). Do not map a 7-session exit silently to a 5/10-session EV-3 cache cell. Only C8 receives implementation investment and may eventually become the monetary owner.

The receipt's `REJECT_THESIS_PRICE`, missing invalidations, absent contracts, quote freshness and liquidity must be investigated as **separate denominators**. The objective is truthful classification and valid coverage, not forcing 1,349 rows to receive a number.

## 6. Downstream behaviour and display

Evening publishes two adjacent states for every ticker: `UNDERLYING_FORECAST` (direction, move/timing distribution, evidence, invalidation, uncertainty) and `OPTION_EXPRESSION` (`BEST_SUPPORTED_LONG_OPTION`, `NO_POSITIVE_EDGE`, `NO_SUITABLE_OPTION`, `NOT_VALUED`, or `PENDING_MORNING_REQUOTE`). Morning republishes the same IDs with a new observation/quote cut, recording thesis change and contract-economics change separately. The Lab and Interpreter display **why** the ticker is expected to move, **whether** an option can monetise it, and which assumptions or missing inputs would change either judgment. A `MONETISABLE` target-conditional calculation or legacy `ev2_*` score is labelled as such; neither may be relabelled calibrated EV.

**Trader-attention invariant.** The Lab's **default landing view is ticker-first**, not a global C8/RAEV sort. It must display one row per ticker with the C5 forecast, evidence/reliability state, expected move window and C6/C8 expression state side by side. Group or filter explicitly into (a) quantified directional forecasts with a valued option, (b) quantified directional forecasts with `NO_SUITABLE_OPTION`/`NO_POSITIVE_EDGE`/`NOT_VALUED`—a prominent *watch for better expression* lane, (c) supported `NEUTRAL_RANGE`, (d) data/statistical insufficiency, and (e) invalidated. No-option forecasts remain searchable and in the default result population; they cannot fall below an expression-only rank cutoff or be hidden by a top-N trade list. Within the forecast view, any C5 priority ordering must come solely from a separately versioned, validated underlying measure; until then use explicit state grouping plus deterministic ticker order, not a counterfeit edge score. A separate **Options Book** may rank valued expressions by shadow RAEV, but its order never changes the forecast view or the published C5 state. The Interpreter starts from the selected ticker forecast, then narrates the best/absent option; it must not infer ticker edge from an option score.

The append-only ledger records all ticker forecasts, every generated/rejected/valued expression, the selected review candidate, human action or non-action, actual fill if any, and later underlying **and exact-option** outcomes as separate labels. Historical counterfactual marks are not actual trade results. Lab/Interpreter joins require run/thesis/expression/OCC and as-of lineage; stale or mismatched joins display a discrepancy, never a fluent repair.
