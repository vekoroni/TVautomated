1. OBJECTIVE AND SCOPE

Provide enough relevant, correctly attributed information for a human to review a ticker thesis and its option expression without loading the whole pipeline into the browser. Preserve auditability, alternative theses and exclusions. The approved reference is the enhanced "Sector overview" mockup; "Focused desk" is a layout alternative, not a separate product to build.

Build the existing Intelligence Lab read path and interface. Do not rebuild Discovery, Vanguard, pricing, databases, ranking or execution policy under the guise of a UI change. No automated orders, sizing, capital permission, provider calls on page load, report generation on expansion, database maintenance or Python-file deletion.

2. GOVERNANCE AND EXPLICIT SUPERSESSION

Read CLAUDE.md; Enhancements/knowledge/README.md; the governing end-to-end specification, especially sections 3, 4, 14, 15, 18, 24 and 25; method notes 03, 05 and 07; and AVS_ANTICIPATED_MOVE_DESIGN_20261003.md, including its later receipts.

ACK business decisions take precedence. For this build:
- Discovery/adopted thesis determines direction; Vanguard enhances and challenges, never silently flips it.
- Quote age alone is disclosure, never a rejection, hiding, ranking penalty or automatic thesis veto. Missing, crossed or mismatched quotes remain separate factual integrity states.
- Human execution remains human. Conditional payoff is a computation, not a trading promise.
- Macro and earnings are review context, not gates, scores or rank inputs.
- Retired R:R, EV3 and legacy expiry-intrinsic payoff are audit models, not competing execution answers.
- Preserve governing setup, Wyckoff phase/event, compression where actually identified, timing, invalidation, historical evidence and counterevidence.

The September specification contains older stale-quote exclusion and 1-20-session language; October decisions and evidence-runway implementations differ. Record the policy/version effective for each run. Do not reintroduce the old quote-age gate, clamp a 54-session source hold to 20, restart clocks, or silently approve a new horizon in this read-model build. Likewise, do not introduce D4's earlier proposed missing-value GO downgrade or a new value ranking without its own approved behavioural change. Show missing value and current producer verdict accurately.

The previous design's proposal to rank in the Lab by anticipated-value multiple is NOT implemented by this design. Presentation consumes published ranking. A user-selected sort is a labelled display order, never a new business rank. Conditional target payoff does not establish expected return or calibrated ranking power.

4. DISPLAY CONTRACT

Home:
- Run/session and publication revision, Evening/Morning state, source times, integrity/missing-data notices.
- Sector name/proxy on every ticker row; sector overview and local filtering.
- Sector daily/weekly state and money flow only from an identified published sector producer, with availability/as-of/method. Candidate distribution is labelled distribution, not rotation or money flow.
- Grid: published rank with basis/status, ticker/sector, side/setup, readiness, move/time, contract/scenario value, review reason. Expiry, hold, spread and earnings are available in row detail; do not make a 25-column home table.
- Counts distinguish underlying theses, selected expressions, ready-for-review states and missing-data states; denominators and filter scope visible. No "executable now" count from quote age or multiple verdict synonyms.

Default ticker card:
1) Thesis/structure/trigger: direction and evidence status; lane and candidate identity; own timeframe/scope; Wyckoff phase/event/status; compression; trigger and latest deciding event; Morning state.
2) Levels/time: qualifying invalidation; separately labelled daily early warning; q50 time-specific level; structural reference; q50/q80 arrival; planned hold/basis; real expiry/calendar days/trading sessions; earnings inside hold/expiry and confirmation.
3) Expression/economics: exact contract, bid/ask/as-of, midpoint spread, delta/IV/OI/volume, premium-at-risk, daily decay and IV sensitivity with units; conditional q50/q80 and earnings stress; target/invalidation/stall returns and pricing/cost basis.
4) Decision/counterevidence/trust: published review verdict and reason, not an additional Lab verdict; thesis state distinct from contract state; conflicts and actionable missing information; quote/bar/stock-price times; EOD reference versus Morning recomputation; journal position state only from actual journal evidence.

Expandable per-ticker drawers:
- Structure, historical evidence, alternative theses (sample size, horizon, original/holdout, estimator, evidence/calibration status).
- Greeks, volatility and cost sensitivities (point-in-time IV-history definition, daily/weekly frequency and sample count).
- Sector, macro and positioning (GEX/walls/PCR are observations, not directions).
- Morning validation, liquidity and chronological stage ladder (events and exclusions; no implied stage pass for missing artifacts).
- Calculation/model conflicts and source audit (purpose, horizon, contract, cost basis, authority and retirement reason).
- Saved Interpreter reports; explicit separately confirmed paid-generation action retained, never triggered by opening the drawer.

No 375-field enumeration, all-row browser duplicate profiling or embedded raw JSON in the default payload. Preserve full evidence artifacts; do not delete history to speed the UI.

5. PUBLICATION AND FAST READ ARCHITECTURE

Produce a versioned lab_review_projection_v1 at the existing governed handoff/publication boundary, using the already available in-memory governed rows. Reuse existing publication machinery rather than introducing a parallel decision engine. This is a presentation record, NOT a replacement decision ledger or thesis.

Projection consists of:
- compact home rows and server-side paging/filter/sort index;
- compact per-row trade-card records;
- per-row references to stage artifacts/evidence indexes;
- independent compact macro/sector context;
- publication manifest: run_id, schema/model/config versions, session, revision, source hashes, population counts, overlay receipt, file hashes, generated_at, publication_state.

Identity is run + candidate/thesis version + expression/contract where present. Never collapse multiple setups/contracts by ticker-only merge. An unresolved row stays visible with its source-row identity and reason; a display diagnostic key is not a fabricated thesis ID. Preserve the complete governed population, not only accepted/actionable rows. Forecast-only and excluded rows use separate indexed views with dispositions, without loading all C5/C6/C8 packets on home.

Apply the same verified overlay/identity semantics as _governed_lab_book. If a manifest or overlay is rejected, expose the diagnostic and the safely available base population as review-only, without granting new action. Do not silently describe fallback as accepted.

Publish generation files first, verify them, atomically replace one manifest pointer last. A client pins the revision and never mixes cards/audit from another revision. A publication failure leaves the previous valid generation available and identifies its date/revision; it must not masquerade as current. Cache by run+revision, with single-flight per generation and bounded memory. No wide-data/hash rebuild per HTTP request.

Proposed endpoints (names are new, not claims of existing routes):
GET /api/lab/<run>/summary -> compact state, counts, sector/macro, publication revision
GET /api/lab/<run>/rows?revision=...&sector=...&page=... -> bounded home rows
GET /api/lab/<run>/rows/<row_key>?revision=... -> compact card
GET /api/lab/<run>/rows/<row_key>/evidence/<family>?revision=... -> that family only
GET /api/lab/<run>/rows/<row_key>/reports?revision=... -> saved reports only

Validate run/row/family parameters; allowlist artifact paths under the exact run; escape all source strings; no browser-provided filesystem path. Lazy audit must have per-row indexes/shards; parsing 470 MB to serve one drawer would move, not solve, the problem.

Compatibility: keep existing full APIs for their known consumers during cutover. They are not default home dependencies. Older runs without a projection show "projection unavailable" and offer an explicit compatibility/audit path. Offline materialisation, if needed, is an explicit release/replay operation, never a paid provider call or hidden cold-request rebuild.

6. ECONOMIC AND SOURCE ALIGNMENT

One quote tuple and one contract identity per valuation. Display ask, IV, spot, as-of, pricing timestamp, rate/dividend basis, model, fee/spread convention and target/time used. No current quote displayed beside a frozen value without an EOD_REFERENCE label. If no Morning quote exists, preserve the EOD value as reference, not current. Repricing unchanged contracts must be tested using today's existing fix.

Spread = (ask-bid)/((ask+bid)/2) * 100, for valid finite non-negative bid, positive ask and bid<=ask; otherwise null with reason. It is a presentation metric, not a change to production spread gates. One formula in the new view; old ask-based figures remain labelled audit.

Keep structural target, volatility reach and the q50 scenario exit spot distinct. q50/q80 refer to the published distribution/test/conditioning, not 50%/80% unconditional success. Value multiple is conditional net proceeds divided by entry cost according to the pricer's cost basis, not EV. Return% = (multiple-1)*100 only when the exact basis agrees. Do not compute expected value by multiplying an in-sample hit rate by one target payoff.

Daily theta and vega sensitivities need explicit premium/contract units, multiplier and IV-point convention. At expiry breakeven is not an early-exit target. Market-implied move is not directional probability. Missing OI/volume is not zero; "no position" requires a successful journal read, not a swallowed exception.

7. BUILD SEQUENCE AND STOP CONDITIONS

S0: Pin inspected code hashes, input revision, current row counts, source mappings and legacy behaviour. Characterise without running providers or mutating live data. Field map and acceptance plan accompany this document.
S1: Projection writer and publication tests. Full row conservation and source ownership; no verdict/rank or model mutation. Begin with failed tests before production edits.
S2: Fast routes, lazy indexed evidence, revision cache and independent macro/sector reads. Test malformed IDs, partial artifacts and concurrent cold requests.
S3: Approved Sector overview UI. Implement exact source mappings; card/evidence uses pinned revision. Forecast and Macro outages cannot block available rows. Detail failure cannot close the ticker view.
S4: Whole-run reconciliation and cold-load measurement on user machine. Reuse existing repricing/scenario fixes, compare against independent raw-input arithmetic, and verify all differences have reasons.
S5: Manual Evening -> Morning -> Lab -> saved Interpreter/journal proving; record model authority and open defects. ACK manually chooses release. No pipeline run or Lab restart without explicit authorization at that stage. No commit/push unless asked.

Stop on a newly required domain-policy change, ambiguous source owner, rejected publication identity, or unexplained row/value mutation. Continue unaffected presentation work and report the precise dependency. A Lab usability release is not certification of predictive profitability.
