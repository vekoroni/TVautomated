## 3. How to read a row

- **Reach / Flat / Inval**: net return on premium at the exit session if spot reaches the vol-budget target (1.5σ), stays flat, or hits the invalidation; entry at ask, exit at modelled bid (ALG-04, friction_model_v1). Scenario values, not forecasts.
- **p\***: the probability of target-before-invalidation you must believe for the trade to break even. Below 0.40 is Tier A. The pipeline's legacy probability is shown beside it; it is uncalibrated.
- **Macro**: ALIGNED / NEUTRAL / AGAINST by sector versus the packet's preferred and avoid lists, plus the calls/puts rule. It is an overlay for the reviewer, never a kill.
- **Morning checklist** (in the CSV): requote two-sided and ≤ 15% of mid with a provider timestamp; spot on the right side of the trigger; invalidation intact; macro overlay read; size human determined.
- **Exit policy**: target or invalidation first; otherwise time-stop at the horizon hold (Day 5 or Day 10); hard last exit at Day 20 or expiry minus the exit buffer, whichever is earlier.

## 4. What this plan is not

Not a pipeline output, not a capital allocation, not a validated probability. The vol forecast is unvalidated (ALG-10 pending) and the reachable payoff scales with it; a 15% forecast error moves the Tier A count by roughly a factor of two.
